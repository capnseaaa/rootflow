Rootflow Toybox

The valley is gray. Wake Sprout and bloom the land. Find Ripple by the pond and bridge the river. Pebble sleeps in the steppe to the east.

Move
Arrow keys or WASD. Hold Shift to run.
Act
Space or Z. Uses your spirit on the tile ahead. Opens chests, reads wrecks, builds homes.
Swap
Q or E picks the next spirit.
Touch
Drag the left pad. Tap A and SWAP.
